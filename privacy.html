No data collection

This webpage does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on a server. There is no backend, no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no cookies.

Local storage only

Your preferences — theme, "show previous buses", favourite routes, and "favourites only" — are saved using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is only used to restore your preferences the next time you open this page. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting

This site is static and served as plain files (for example, via GitHub Pages). The hosting provider may keep its own standard server logs for operating the service; the site itself adds nothing on top of that.

Third parties

This site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or embeds.

Changes

This page may be updated at any time as the site evolves.
